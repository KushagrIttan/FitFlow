What Is This?

A local-first wardrobe app that recommends daily outfits based on weather, style, and wardrobe rotation. You add clothes (photos, categories, warmth levels), and it suggests complete outfits — a top, bottom, shoes, and optional accessory. It has a Gemini AI "Stylist" mode that picks the best combos and explains why they work, with graceful fallback to local scoring when offline.

No cloud backend, no accounts, no data leaving the device. Your wardrobe lives in a local SQLite database. This is privacy-first done right.

The Recommendation Engine — A Closer Look

This is the beating heart of the app, and it's thoughtfully designed.

How it works:

  1. Filter — Remove items in laundry, incompatible with weather warmth, or home-only (when going out). Exclude upper-body items worn yesterday (wardrobe rotation).
  2. Score — For each item: daysSinceWorn × 0.3 − wearCount × 0.5 + ratingBoost × 0.3. This favors items you haven't worn recently, penalizes overused ones, and boosts items you've rated highly.
  3. Permute — Generate all valid upper × lower × footwear × (optional accessory) combinations. In wet weather, jackets/hoodies/sweaters get a +2 bonus.
  4. Pick top 5 — Highest-scoring combos sent to Gemini for stylist selection.
  5. Gemini returns — Picks 2-3 combos with color/weather/vibe justifications. Falls back to local top 3 on any error.

The O(N³) permutation is fine for a personal wardrobe (10 uppers × 10 lowers × 8 shoes = 800 combos — trivial). The genius is that Gemini never sees your entire wardrobe, just the pre-filtered top 5. This keeps API costs near-zero and avoids token-limit issues.
